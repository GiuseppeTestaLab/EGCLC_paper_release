# Additional DNA Methylation Analyses (Revision Stage)

During the revision process of the manuscript, we added **DNA methylation profiling (EM-seq)** of **hEGCLCs** at later passages (p10).  

This folder contains all analyses including these new samples, in addition to the previously reported ones.  

## Contents

1. [**1.ExploratoryAnalysis**](./1.ExploratoryAnalysis)  
   Initial quality checks, dimensionality reduction, and exploratory visualization of methylation profiles of all samples together.  

2. [**2.DifferentialAnalysis**](./2.DifferentialAnalysis)  
   Differentially methylated region (DMR) detection between new EGCLCs p10 samples and the other samples.  

3. [**3.DMRAnnotation**](./3.DMRAnnotation)  
   Genomic annotation of identified DMRs.  

4. [**4.DMRInterpretation**](./4.DMRInterpretation)  
   Exploration of the most significant DMRs and of genes of interest.  

5. [**5.ImprintedExploration**](./5.ImprintedExploration)  
   Analysis of methylation patterns at known imprinted regions.  

6. [**6.EscapeesExploration**](./6.EscapeesExploration)  
   Investigation of methylation patterns in Tang et al. escapee regions.  

## Notes
- These analyses extend the results from the main manuscript.  
- For reproducibility, scripts and results are organized consistently with the main analysis pipeline.
